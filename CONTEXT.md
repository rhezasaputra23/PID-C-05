# PID-C-05 Fleet Telematics

This context defines the domain language for the PID-C-05 city-bus telemetry project set in Malang.

## Language

**Bus**: A simulated city vehicle assigned to one of the project's four routes.

**Passenger capacity**: The simulation limit of 40 passengers aboard one bus; occupancy is the number currently onboard.

**Route**: An ordered corridor of stops that a bus follows in the Malang simulation.

**Route corridor**: The geographic path a bus is expected to follow between and through its route's stops.

**Stop**: A scheduled passenger boarding point with a geographic coordinate and geofence.

**Geofence**: The circular area around a stop used to recognize bus arrival and departure.

**Stop visit**: One bus's arrival at a stop and its subsequent departure, producing one arrival and one departure event.

**Route deviation**: A bus position more than 200 metres from its assigned route corridor for two consecutive valid GPS readings.

**Late arrival**: A stop arrival more than five minutes after that stop's scheduled arrival time.

**Dirty data**: Simulator-injected missing coordinates, GPS jumps, duplicate messages, or late/out-of-order messages that the pipeline must detect, handle, and count.

**Backfill**: Reproducible historical data generated by the simulator for the required seven-day warehouse period.

**Backfill rate**: The default historical generation rate of one message per bus per minute; real-time demonstration mode uses one message per second per bus.

**Real-time mode**: Simulator mode that publishes bus telemetry at the target live rate for the dashboard demonstration.

**Current position**: The latest valid, non-late GPS position accepted for a bus.

**On-time rate**: The share of scheduled stop arrivals on a route that occur no more than five minutes after schedule.

**Missed stop**: A scheduled stop with no bus arrival recorded within ten minutes after its scheduled arrival time; it counts as not on time.
